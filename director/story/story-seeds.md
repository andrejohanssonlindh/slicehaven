## Seed A — "The Dead Don't Rest" (main arc)
Northern Asharien, a dry year. The party are hired swords in Jarla. Undead are leaving the Dead Forest in numbers never seen before, and the villages along the river are emptying. The lord of Jarla wants it quiet (it's bad for trade); the local knights want to burn the forest; a scholar from the university quietly pays well for "samples".
- Act 1: defend the villages, find out the dead are being *called*, not wandering.
- Pause point: to enter the deep forest you need the elves' leave, so you must do something for them first (side chain).
- Act 2: a way down beneath the forest — the Underworld.
- Grey choices: the scholar may be the only one who understands it, and also the one who caused it.

## Seed B — "Bread and Iron"
The famine drives everything. Grain convoys, river pirates, a renegade mercenary captain who feeds his men by raiding. Do you fight him, or join him? Very Eon: no clear good side.

## Seed C — Combine
A as the main arc, B's factions as side quests that make the main arc easier or harder (e.g. if you crushed the mercenaries, the north is safer but there are fewer swords when the dead come).
