# World notes — Eon / Mundana

*My own summary for our use, from a read of the world chapter. No text from the books goes into the game. See Q1 in [questions](../questions.md) about how directly we use names.*

## Tone to borrow
- **Grimy realism, grey morality.** No absolute good or evil: just people, their agendas and the consequences. Death comes as easily to a farmer as to a princess.
- **History everywhere.** Empires rose and fell; ruins, legends and lost knowledge in every corner. Perfect for scenario sites.
- **The fantastic stays strange.** Dragons, sorcerers, undead and living gods exist, but they're rare and frightening, not everyday.
- Other worlds (the Shadowland, the Realm of the Dead) work on dream logic. Good for late-campaign weirdness.

## The five realms in the core book
| Realm | One-line feel | Game use |
|---|---|---|
| **Asharien** | Arid, unruly union of ten city-states; red steppe, famine, bandits, stubbornly free people | **Recommended starting region** |
| Damarien | Isolated, gloomy grand duchy under an iron-fisted ruler; cults, undead, dark forests | Great "act 2" region |
| Forion | Tropical theocracy under a reincarnated god | Exotic later region |
| Jargian Empire | Huge creaking empire: legions, the Daak church, corrupt bureaucracy | Antagonist power / politics |
| Mûhad | Ancient desert theocracy, art, trade and sorcery | Later region |

## Why Asharien to start
- Small, varied, full of trouble: drought and famine, a renegade mercenary captain raiding the north, pirate wars, a haunted forest left over from an old necromancer's war, a university city where magic experiments go wrong.
- Freedom-loving people and mixed peoples (humans, elves from the western forest, dwarves, misslor) → easy to justify a mixed party.
- Lots of natural scenario types: bandit camps, river pirates, ruins, a haunted forest, a gladiator city, refugee camps, border forts.

## Places I'd like to use (in our own words)
- **Jarla** — northern border city on the great river, run by a corrupt, crowd-pleasing lord. Good hub for the start.
- **Döda skogen (the Dead Forest)** — haunted forest by the elven woods; undead keep stumbling out, and nobody knows why new ones keep appearing. A main-arc mystery.
- **Chadarians hopp** — university city, rival magic academies, secret agendas. Good for side quests and a sorcerer hero.
- **Nimto bro** — violent city of mercenaries and a gladiator arena. Arena scenarios!
- **Daggbacken** — villages of the small folk (misslor) along a river. A gentle place to protect.
- **Camard** — the capital and port; merchant princes, refugees outside the walls. A possible town/home base.
