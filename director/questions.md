## Q35 · Rolling sight (B5)
Sight becomes an Alertness roll at key moments (a new area, every 6 hours, waking), setting the cone's reach and width until the next roll.
- **Shown or hidden?** **A.** Hidden; only the cone changes. *(My pick: the game shouldn't stop for dice every six hours.)* **B.** Shown as a small roll (icons). **C.** Shown only at the dice level you've chosen, like every other roll.
- **How big a swing?** My pick: a good roll gives up to about +25 % reach and a little width, a bad one down to about −25 %, around today's value from Alertness. Bigger if you want it to be felt.

## Q34 · Inventory: fitting things on a phone, and weight (B6)
- **Placing items:** a grid you drag in (Tarkov, Resident Evil) is fiddly on a portrait phone. **A.** Tap an item to pick it up, tap a cell to put it down, two-finger twist (or a button) to turn it, plus an **auto-pack** button. *(My pick.)* **B.** Drag and drop as well. **C.** Only auto-pack; shapes matter for what fits, not for hand-arranging.
- **Weight:** the ruleset gives gear in kilograms and gives *armour* an encumbrance that raises base fatigue (its table), but I find no rule turning *carried kilograms* into encumbrance. Proposal: a free load that grows with build (Physique), then **1 encumbrance per kilogram over it**, fed into the same table. Or do you know a rule I've missed (page)?

## Q33 · B3 as a frame, built in slices, and the new order (B3, B6)
You're right that B3 is large. My proposal ([sketch](design/actions.md)): **every action goes through one pipeline**, offer → gates (allowed? tools? place? state?) → resolve (no roll, one roll, a read, a challenge, a social conflict, later crafting) → outcome (a state, time, an item, a flag, how someone sees you). A failed *permission* gate doesn't block: it makes the act a transgression that a witness may notice. We build it in slices:
1. **B3a** the frame + lighting a fire, sitting, the reads we have (owners stubbed: everything allowed)
2. **B6** inventory (shapes, pockets, bag, add-ons, weight) and **B3b** take and carry (size classes; a ladder is carried in the hands)
3. **B4** sheets on people, **B5** perception with the rolled sight
4. **B3c** owners and transgressions, **B3d** talking in 3D (leave to use the forge)
5. **B7** effects, **B10** crafting (new: your smith), then combat and magic

And your way of growing it, 10–20 % now, then you send **action notes** that become data files, is the smart way: the frame is what makes that cheap. **Agree, or change the order?** One thing to weigh: inventory before people means you can take things before anyone can object; stealing has no consequence until B3c.
