### Q38 · The scale of how people see you (holds up B3c)
Your notes suggest one scale, about −100 to +100, that each act moves and where each consequence starts. Who holds it?

- **B, each person and each place from the start.** A village has a reputation for you as well, and strangers there start from it. It does more at once, but it's harder to tune before we've seen A work.
