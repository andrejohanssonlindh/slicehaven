# Director's notes, 2026-11-03 (second set: what can be said, and hooks)

*Original text kept verbatim below, then an English digest. Where it went: [openings for talk](../design/talk-openings.md) (a design for discussion), Q40 and Q41.*

## Original

> Talk log is hidden behind the social buttons.
>
> We need to consider what skills are visible at the start. I think this interface needs an external hook. Like there should absolutely be stuff one can talk to non quest giving peasants for without a specific hook, but most things i think should require one. Comfort an npc? Why? Certainly if something happened?
>
> Using law etc might be useful in a challenge (e.g stealing the chair) but not without a hook. I also think perhaps you could by finding things around you in the world and through quests could collect hooks. I am not perfectly sure how this would work yet but lets document and discuss it some.
>
> Also the guard, kinte, i only see the top of his head.

## Digest
- **Two faults:** the talk log hides behind the choices; the guard's portrait shows only the top of his head. (Fixed the same day.)
- **Not every try should be offered.** Some things you can talk about with anyone, even a peasant with no quest. Most tries need a **reason, a hook from outside**: comforting someone makes sense only if something has happened to them; arguing law makes sense in a situation (taking the chair), not out of the blue.
- **Hooks could be collected**, by finding things in the world and through quests. How exactly is open: document it and talk it through.
