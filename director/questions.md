## Q47 Two short after-effects from one blow (B8)
Some wound rows give two short effects at once, like *dazed* and *drops*. Each is −1D6 until the end of your next active phase. The book doesn't say whether two of them add up.
- **A (what I've built):** they add up: dazed and drops together are −2D6. The same effect twice doesn't add; it just lasts.
- **B:** only −1D6 however many there are.

## Q46 The first combat vignette (B8a)
I propose a **sparring match in the arena** (`?map=arena`): one sparring partner, melee only, and each round you choose your tactic. You see the rolls, a fight log and your injuries. A **mark** (1 advantage spent on showing you could have struck) or being knocked out ends it, and **no one dies in sparring**, so you can try it again and again. Death comes in fights out in the world (B8f). **A:** yes, start there. **B:** something else (say what).

## Q45 The rules' distance bands in metres (B8)
The book uses five bands. In metres I propose: **adjacent** up to 2 m (melee), **short** 2–10 m, **medium** 10–25 m, **long** 25–50 m, **very long** past 50 m. Moving one band in a round becomes **walking up to 5 m**; two bands (a movement roll) becomes **running up to 10 m**. A round is 4 seconds. **A:** yes. **B:** other numbers.
