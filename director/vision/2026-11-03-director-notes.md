## Digest
- **B6 (inventory) is not critical**, so it moves later. The carried-weight rule waits until the director has looked in the books.
- **Talking to someone brings up a portrait** and the conversation log; the choices are laid over it. → B3d.
- **Taking a thing is many actions, not one.** It works for most objects, not only the stair:
  - **argue** that it's yours to take, or **threaten**: a challenge;
  - **borrow** it: your standing drops if you don't bring it back. Is there a way to return things? Might some people (those who like order) bring a found thing back themselves?
  - just **move** it: the owner gets upset if it goes too far (out of the house?); still a challenge;
  - **buy** it: a challenge;
  - **just take it**, no talk ("I'll use this as a weapon"): no roll, plainly a transgression;
  - **steal it unseen** when no one is around: a challenge, but an easy one when alone. The owner **recognises** their chair carried down the street, but not something in a pocket. Hiding it is a roll whose difficulty comes from its **size**, your **clothes** and how you **pack** it.
  - → B3b, B3c.
- **The owner's view of you moves** with what you do. Past some point they fetch the guards or willing villagers, or start a fight. Where that point lies depends on **your** physique, the weapons you show and traits such as scars (a passive), and on **their** own leaning: a tirak likely fights, a human less so. → B3c.
- **Consequences depend on where you are and who you are.** Nobility gets off lightly, a tirak the opposite, unless you are a trukh and everyone is afraid. One **scale**, perhaps −100 to +100, runs from a slap on the wrist to everyone trying to kill you. Each culture sets how much a transgression moves it and where each consequence starts. → B3c.
- **The item's value** makes the action harder and the consequence worse, weighed against **the owner's wealth** (a lord won't miss a hen). **Perceived importance** counts too: try to steal a guard's sword and you are in for it. → B3c.
- **B3a works well so far.**
