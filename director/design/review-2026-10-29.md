# Code review: the 3D world from M2 on (2026-10-29)

*Asked for by the director: a general review of the 3D work since M2 (layouts, props, houses streaming, sight, filaments, weather, the inn, shop and stable, stairs, walking). Looking for what is wrong now and what will hurt later. Nothing below has been fixed yet; the order at the end is my proposal.*

**Verdict:** the core (`src/core/world3d/`) is in good shape: pure, deterministic, tested, and built to scale (sparse nav grid, lazy filament field, chunked placed things, a house index). The weak spot is `src/engine3d/world.ts`. It has grown to 2,400 lines and now holds game rules (walking, stairs, the innkeeper's routine, who is in which house) mixed in with the drawing. That is the one thing to fix before M5 (people on routes), because every new person would go into the same file.

**The main plan now lives in [How the world is put together](world-structure.md)** (kinds of thing in a tree, maps as files, doing things to things). Items 8 and 9 below are steps of it. The rest of this review is fixes.

## Bugs (they happen now)

1. ✅ **Fixed 2026-10-29 by item 4** (test: `e2e/spike3d-storeys.e2e.ts`, which fails on the old code). **The hero drops to the ground floor if the view is dragged away.** Chunks stream round the *camera's* focus, not the hero. Drag about 50 m away and the hero's house is taken down; `heroHouse()` then says "outdoors", and `stepHero` resets the storey to 0. When the house comes back the hero is on the ground floor, possibly inside a partition. A walk in progress also keeps pointing at the house object that was taken down. *Fix: keep the chunk the hero (and anyone mid-walk) is in loaded, and keep the hero's house as the map's `PlacedHouse` plus a storey number, not the renderer's object.*
2. ✅ **Fixed 2026-10-29 by item 4.** **The innkeeper has the same weakness.** Their walk holds the renderer's house object. If the inn streams out and back while they are on the stairs, they carry on with the old one. It is rare now, and the same fix covers it.
3. ✅ **Fixed 2026-10-30** (tests: `tests/fire.test.ts`, and the GPU geometry count in `e2e/spike3d-fx.e2e.ts`, which fails on the old code): shadow twins share one material; flames, the hand torch, the orb and the ray let go of their geometry and material. **Leaks when walking a big map.** Each time a house streams in, every cut-away part makes a shadow-only `MeshBasicMaterial` that is never let go. Each torch post's flame makes a material that `emptyChunk` doesn't dispose either. Campfires (`douseFires`), the hand torch and the orb are removed without disposing anything. On `?map=wilds` walking up and down Eastgate this grows without end. Each one is small; together they will show on a phone after a long session.

**✅ Fixed 2026-10-30 (it was the test, not the engine: the palisade across the village at z = 18 stood on the test's sight line; it now looks through the open gate).** **Also: a browser test is failing on `main`.** `e2e/spike3d-vision.e2e.ts`, "a torch is seen from far beyond plain sight at night". The far torch is in `sightLights`, but `seen()` at the torch's own spot is 0 (it should be over 0.2). It fails the same way on the commit before today's cleanup, so one of the recent 3D changes broke it, and nobody noticed because the e2e tests take about 8 minutes and aren't run before every push. *Fix it, and run the 3D e2e before any push that touches `engine3d`, as CLAUDE.md asks.*

## Things that will hurt later

4. ✅ **Done 2026-10-29:** `core/world3d/actors.ts` has `Actor` (plain data, houses by id), `Walking` (plan, step, steer, stairs, `settle` at doors) and `Rounds` (the keeper's routine, `keeperRounds`/`stepRounds`); `SpikeWorld.hero` is an `Actor` and `world.moves` its `Walking`. Unit tests: `tests/actors.test.ts`. Map ids are now checked unique (`compileMap` problem). **Rules live in the renderer.** `planWalk`, the walk legs, the stairs state (`level`, `heroFloor`, `onStair`, `legs`, `legI`, `legS`, `wasIn`, `steering`), key steering, the innkeeper's stops and the villager's beat are all private parts of `SpikeWorld`. So they can't be unit-tested (vitest has no WebGL; only the e2e screenshots touch them). The hero's state can't go into a save either: it holds three.js objects, which breaks the "state is plain JSON" rule. *Proposal: a pure `core/world3d/actors.ts` with an `Actor` ({x, z, house id, storey, foot height, stair, legs}), `planWalk` over the house index and the per-storey nav grids, and `stepActor(dt)`. The hero, the innkeeper and every M5 villager use it, and the renderer only places their cards. This is the step that makes "people on routes" cheap.*
5. *(Half done: walking no longer uses them; the public calls below still do.)* **House numbers leak out of the API.** `heroHouse()`, `pick`, `houseTapped`, `toggleRoof` and `walkIn` take or return an index into `world.houses`, but those indexes change every time a house streams in or out (the handover already warns about it). Something will hold one across a frame and point at the wrong house. *Return the house itself (or its id).*
6. **The memory that grows with the world walked.** `HouseIndex.lites` and the model cache in `layout.ts` keep every house model ever looked at, meshes included, forever. Only the furnished rooms are forgotten. The nav grid's blocks and the field's ground and indoor caches also never shrink (smaller, but the same kind of growth). For a city of hundreds of houses this is the one place where "nothing is sized by the world" stops being true. *Forget lites the way furnished rooms are forgotten; let the nav grid forget blocks far away.*
7. **The filament field forgets everything twice a second.** `feedField` calls `setConditions` and `setMovers`, and each clears the whole cache, so every reading is worked out again each half second even when only the hero took a step. It's fine while only the heat map reads it. Once spells and NPC senses read it, it won't be. *Clear only the cells within reach of a mover's old and new spot; clear everything only when the weather really changes.*
8. ✅ *(Done 2026-10-30: a building plan is data, `BuildingPlan` in `core/world3d/house.ts`, one entry per kind in `content/things/kinds/buildings.ts`; `buildHouse` has no `if (plan === …)` left, and every plan builds byte-for-byte as before. A new kind of building is a new entry.)* **Building kinds are code branches.** `buildHouse` handles cottage, inn, shop and stable with `if (plan === ...)` branches in one 500-line function. A smithy, temple, manor or longhouse each means more branches. That runs against "authoring by intent". *Before the fourth kind, turn a plan into data (storeys and their heights, room shares and roles, where the stairs go) that one builder reads.*
9. ✅ *(Done 2026-10-30: the cast is `MapSpec.people`, kinds of person with a beat or the rounds of a house; the door torch is a `walltorch` the map asks for; the hero's look is a person in `content/spike3d.ts` until the creator gives one.)* **The world class assumes the spike.** The hero's and villager's looks, the door torch on `layout.houses[0]`, the innkeeper on "the first house with stairs" and the villager's beat are all hard-wired in `SpikeWorld`. They should come from the map (`MapSpec` people with routines), so a second map doesn't inherit the hamlet's cast.
10. **One map rule lives only in memory.** Sprites assume a house's front faces the camera, so a bed or a horse in a house that faces away looks wrong. The map compiler should report that as a `problem`, so a map can't break it by accident.

## Smaller things

11. **Per-frame garbage:** `update` makes new colours (`lin()` ×6, `fogC.clone()`), maps and sorts every torch, filters and sorts people (`updateSee`), and rebuilds two arrays in every `heroHouse()` call (about eight a frame). Each costs little, but phones feel garbage collection. Tidy it when touching that code.
12. **Unreachable taps** search a box at least 40 m round both ends; on fresh ground that can build about 100 nav blocks in one frame (a hitch). Cap the search or spread it over frames.
13. `Math.random` drives lightning (`strike`, `autoBolts`), and a strike leaves a scorch that feeds the filament field. So rule state comes from an unseeded source. That's fine for a test page, but use the seeded `Rng` once weather matters to the rules.
14. `MapSpec.wild.where` is a function, so a map isn't pure data and can't be stored or edited as JSON. Fine until there is a map editor.

## Also done today: the 2D engine removed
The hex sandbox, the gate scene page, Reedwater, the Art Lab and the Director's "Village art" tab are gone. Everything still runs from the git tag `2d-archive`, and the art is kept as pictures in the [archive](../archive/2d-art.md). The 3D world no longer depends on any hex code: the filament rules have their own cell type, the weather moved to `core/world3d/weather.ts`, and the ground painter no longer reads the hex tile.
