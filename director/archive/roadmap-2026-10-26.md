# Roadmap

*Design stage. No fixed milestones: the engine comes first, in vignettes. Each vignette ends with something playable on the phone. Order is a proposal.*

## Done
- Foundations: repo, deploy, Director's Room, seeded RNG, hex maths.
- Engine sandbox: camera, weather, time, wind, fire, lightning, filaments, turns.
- Character creator.
- Gate scene: skills on the world, social conflicts, things bigger than one hex.

## Next up
See the [ranked backlog](backlog.md): skill web shape + Search fix, then creator event cards and pre-set characters, then humanoid social skills.

## Candidate vignettes
- **A fight** under the ruleset: initiative, actions, damage, Focus. What does a round feel like with D6 pools?
- **Tapping the ground:** a small menu (walk / look / use a skill) and look lines for every ground type and prop (the "almost everything is clickable" principle).
- **Magic in play:** one mystic skill on the map, using the filaments (see [magic on the map](magic-on-map.md)).
- **Free-flowing time** in a scene: something that moves while you decide.
- **Equipment in play:** armour, weapons and encumbrance doing something in the sandbox.
- **A second scene** with a different feel (indoors, a market, a night watch) to test how reusable the scene data is.

## Later, once the engine is clearer
- Campaign structure and map, party traits, progression.
- Story ([parked](../questions.md)).
- Final art style, then music and sound.
- Translation files (groundwork in `src/i18n/`).
